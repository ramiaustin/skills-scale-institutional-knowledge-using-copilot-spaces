# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical direction, technical planning, and cross-team execution alignment. They help convert product intent into achievable technical delivery plans while supporting team health and execution quality.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and engineering direction
- Sequence work across teams and dependencies
- Support estimation, prioritization, and technical trade-off decisions
- Help unblock delivery risks and resolve cross-team coordination issues
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on feasibility and milestone planning

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound and sustainable solutions
- Maintain delivery momentum across dependencies and shared services
- Improve engineering predictability and execution quality

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and architecture reviews
- Dependency mapping and milestone alignment
- Cross-functional delivery discussions with PMs and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to translate goals into feasible technical plans
- Coordinates with Project Managers on sequencing, risks, and milestone readiness
- Advises Developers on technical direction, quality standards, and implementation trade-offs

### Outcomes Improved
- Better technical prioritization
- Clearer ownership of complex dependencies
- Reduced delivery risk from unclear implementation paths

---

## Quality / QA Leads

### Role Summary
Quality / QA Leads define quality strategy, validation approaches, and release readiness criteria. They ensure that delivery meets both functional and non-functional expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy across unit, integration, and end-to-end coverage
- Establish acceptance validation approaches and quality gates
- Review release readiness against business and technical criteria
- Partner with Developers and Product Managers on defect triage and regression prevention
- Monitor recurring quality issues and suggest corrective actions

### Goals
- Improve confidence in release quality
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Align validation with customer experience and acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and QA signoff sessions
- Defect triage and release readiness reviews
- Cross-team validation checkpoints before milestone completion

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers to review testability and coverage
- Partners with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and validation evidence
- Works with Project Managers to ensure quality checkpoints are included in schedules

### Outcomes Improved
- Higher confidence before release
- Clearer definition of “done”
- Better alignment between product expectations and validation outcomes

---

## Security / Compliance Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance reviewers assess risks, privacy requirements, and policy obligations as part of project delivery. They help teams ship responsibly while minimizing operational and compliance exposure.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate product or service risks related to security, privacy, and compliance
- Review architecture and implementation choices for control gaps
- Recommend mitigations and remediation steps
- Support project teams with policy and requirement interpretation
- Participate in review gate checks for high-risk changes

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Ensure delivery aligns with internal policies and regulatory expectations
- Build trust in release readiness for sensitive or customer-facing work

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and architecture check-ins
- Policy guidance and risk discussions
- Incident or exposure assessment updates when relevant

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Engineering Leads to review technical designs and mitigations
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk trade-offs and release gating
- Coordinates with Developers to ensure secure implementation patterns are followed

### Outcomes Improved
- Safer delivery practices
- Clear risk ownership and escalation paths
- Better compliance readiness for launches and production changes

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaisons translate customer feedback, stakeholder priorities, and adoption concerns into actionable project inputs. They make sure delivery work remains tied to user value and stakeholder needs.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and summarize customer feedback, concerns, and requests
- Identify stakeholder priorities, dependencies, and communication needs
- Help validate whether outcomes are meeting stakeholder expectations
- Surface adoption, usability, or support issues that should influence roadmap decisions
- Coordinate updates and feedback loops across teams

### Goals
- Keep delivery aligned with customer and stakeholder value
- Improve communication and trust across functions
- Turn external feedback into actionable product direction

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and feedback reviews
- Customer issue escalations and adoption discussions
- Cross-functional planning and milestone check-ins

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with market and customer insight
- Supports Project Managers with stakeholder communication and dependency awareness
- Shares adoption signals with Developers and Quality teams so product decisions remain grounded in real usage

### Outcomes Improved
- Stronger customer value alignment
- Better stakeholder engagement
- Improved feedback loops between delivery and users

---

## Release Managers / Delivery Operations

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Delivery Operations partners coordinate deployment windows, release readiness, and communication across teams. They reduce operational risk by aligning release activity with support and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release timing and deployment windows
- Validate release checklists, rollback readiness, and operational impacts
- Communicate release status to stakeholders and support teams
- Align pre-release validation with engineering, QA, and product owners
- Support incident response coordination during release-related issues

### Goals
- Increase release predictability and stability
- Reduce operational disruption during deployment
- Maintain clear communication before, during, and after launches

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews
- Deployment status updates
- Stakeholder and support communications before and after release

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on release plans and sequencing
- Coordinates with Engineering Leads and QA Leads on readiness checks
- Partners with Product Managers to ensure communication aligns with release goals and user impact

### Outcomes Improved
- Better deployment discipline
- Lower operational surprises
- Clearer stakeholder communication around delivery timing

---

## Data / Analytics Partners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Partners measure impact, validate whether goals are being achieved, and guide continuous learning. They make project outcomes more evidence-based by connecting delivery activity to measurable business and user impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track success metrics and leading indicators
- Help validate whether product or project changes are delivering expected impact
- Support experiments, instrumentation, and analysis planning
- Partner with product and leadership teams on trend interpretation
- Surface evidence that informs iteration and prioritization

### Goals
- Improve evidence-based decision making
- Measure value and outcomes objectively
- Help teams learn quickly from delivery results

### Typical Communication
- Metric reviews and dashboard check-ins
- Analysis summaries for planning and retrospective sessions
- Alignment meetings tied to roadmap and release impact

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Product Managers assess whether goals are being met
- Provides Project Managers with status signals tied to delivery outcomes
- Supports Engineering and QA teams in understanding quality, usage, and performance impacts

### Outcomes Improved
- Better prioritization based on evidence
- Clearer measurement of project value
- Stronger learning loops and continuous improvement

---

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify how cross-functional teams coordinate across planning, execution, quality, release, and stakeholder communication.
